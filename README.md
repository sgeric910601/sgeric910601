## English

### About me

I'm a master's student in Hydraulic Engineering at the Department of Civil Engineering, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), working on **rockfall detection and early warning** for slopes.
I also build small macOS / Linux tools to make everyday things work better.

### Research: rockfall detection & early warning

- **Rockfall tracking in drone footage** — classic OpenCV image processing that finds rocks only a few pixels wide in hovering-drone video and tracks their paths, with a PySide6 desktop GUI
- **Rockfall warning light system** — a warning-light control service, LINE / Discord bot notifications, and physical warning lights on ESP8266 / ESP32
- **Seismic data processing** — tools to convert SmartSolo recordings to SAC and remove instrument response
